## Key Clauses

1. **Term & Renewal** — This Agreement automatically renews for successive 12-month periods unless either Party gives at least 60 days' written notice of non-renewal.

2. **Termination for Convenience** — Either Party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice to the other Party.

3. **Termination for Cause** — Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other Party commits a material breach that remains unremedied 14 days after written notice, or becomes insolvent.

4. **Confidentiality** — Each Party shall keep the other's confidential information private and shall not disclose it to any third party, for a period of 3 years following termination.

5. **Limitation of Liability** — Each Party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed £120,000 or the fees paid in the preceding 12 months, whichever is greater. Neither Party shall be liable for indirect or consequential loss.

6. **Governing Law** — This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
