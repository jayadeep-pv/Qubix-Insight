## 1. Scope of Services

1.1 Hartley & Webb shall design, develop and implement a bespoke Legal Practice Management System ("System") for the Client in accordance with the specification set out in Schedule 1.

1.2 The project shall be delivered in four phases over an estimated timeline of 18 months from the project commencement date ("Commencement Date"), with milestones as set out in Schedule 2.

1.3 Hartley & Webb shall provide post-go-live support and maintenance for a period of 12 months following system acceptance.

---

## 2. Charges and Payment Terms

2.1 The total fixed price for implementation of the System is £485,000 exclusive of VAT, payable in accordance with the milestone payment schedule in Schedule 2.

2.2 Invoices shall be issued upon completion of each milestone as certified by Hartley & Webb's project manager.

2.3 Payment terms: Net 45 days from date of invoice.

2.4 Hartley & Webb reserves the right to charge interest on overdue invoices at 8% above the Bank of England base rate.

2.5 Any variations to the agreed scope of work shall be charged at Hartley & Webb's standard day rates, currently £1,250 per consultant day.

---

## 3. Liability

3.1 Hartley & Webb's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total charges paid by the Client in the twelve months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

3.2 Based on the contract value of £485,000 spread over 18 months, this equates to an approximate cap of £323,333 in year one, reducing thereafter.

> Note: this clause will result in an effective liability cap of approximately £323,333 — well below the total contract value of £485,000. Client's legal team to review.

3.3 Hartley & Webb excludes all liability for loss of profits, loss of data, loss of business, and all indirect or consequential loss howsoever caused.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Both parties agree to keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party during the term of this Agreement and for a period of two (2) years following termination.

## 7. Termination

7.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on giving 90 days' written notice to Hartley & Webb, provided that the Client shall remain liable for all work completed and costs incurred up to the date of termination plus a cancellation fee of 25% of the remaining outstanding contract value.

7.2 Hartley & Webb may terminate this Agreement immediately if: (a) the Client fails to make payment within 30 days of the due date; or (b) the Client commits an act of insolvency.

---

## 8. Warranty

8.1 Hartley & Webb warrants that the System will perform materially in accordance with the agreed specification for a period of 90 days following final system acceptance ("Warranty Period").

8.2 During the Warranty Period, Hartley & Webb shall remedy defects at no additional charge. Following expiry of the Warranty Period, remediation shall be charged at standard day rates.

---

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any dispute shall first be referred to senior management for resolution. Failing resolution within 30 days, disputes shall be referred to binding arbitration under the LCIA rules.

## Schedule 1 — System Specification (Summary)

- Case management module for litigation, corporate and property matters
- Time recording and billing integrated with existing accounts software
- Client portal with secure document sharing
- Regulatory compliance module (SRA, AML, GDPR)
- Management reporting and KPI dashboards
- Integration with Microsoft 365 and existing document management system
- Mobile application for fee earners (iOS and Android)
