# SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement Reference:** CPF-SA-2024-0112
**Date:** 14 March 2024
**Version:** Final — Execution Copy

---

## Parties

**Service Provider:** ClearPath Freight Solutions Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 10293847), having its registered office at Unit 7 Meridian Business Park, Leicester, LE19 1WZ ("ClearPath")

**Client:** Harrowfield & Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (OC No. 312847), having its principal place of business at One Aldersgate Street, London, EC2A 4HJ ("Client")

---

## 1. Services

1.1 ClearPath agrees to provide nationwide document courier, logistics and records management services as more particularly described in Schedule 1 attached hereto ("Services").

1.2 The Services shall commence on 1 April 2024 and shall continue for an initial term of two (2) years, with automatic annual renewal unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the renewal date ("Initial Term").

1.3 ClearPath shall assign a dedicated Senior Relationship Manager and backup contact to the Client within two (2) business days of the commencement date.

---

## 2. Charges and Payment Terms

2.1 The Client shall pay ClearPath the charges set out in Schedule 2, which amounts to £195,000 per annum exclusive of VAT.

2.2 ClearPath shall issue invoices monthly in arrears on the last working day of each calendar month, accompanied by a full itemised activity report.

2.3 Payment terms: Net 28 days from the date of invoice. All payments shall be made by BACS transfer or CHAPS to the bank account notified by ClearPath in writing.

2.4 Interest shall accrue on overdue amounts at 2% per annum above the Bank of England base rate, calculated daily.

2.5 Charges shall be fixed for the Initial Term. Any increase thereafter shall be subject to a minimum of 90 days' written notice and shall not exceed 3% per annum.

---

## 3. Liability

3.1 Neither party excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability which cannot be excluded or limited by applicable law.

3.2 ClearPath's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement, whether arising in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall in no event exceed £2,000,000.

3.3 Neither party shall be liable to the other for any loss of profits, loss of business, loss of goodwill, loss of anticipated savings, or any indirect or consequential loss, save where such loss results from wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

---

## 4. Data Protection

4.1 The parties acknowledge that ClearPath acts as a data processor on behalf of the Client (as data controller) in respect of personal data processed in the course of providing the Services.

4.2 Each party shall comply in all respects with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. A Data Processing Agreement is attached as Schedule 3.

4.3 GDPR compliance clause: Included. ClearPath has appointed a Data Protection Officer (registration ref: ZA874521) contactable at dpo@clearpathfreight.co.uk. ClearPath maintains ISO 27001 certification (certificate no. IS-1042938).

4.4 ClearPath shall notify the Client within 24 hours of becoming aware of any personal data breach that may affect Client data, and shall provide a full written incident report within 72 hours.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and not to disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent.

5.2 ClearPath shall ensure all staff with access to Client documents have signed individual confidentiality agreements prior to commencing work.

5.3 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All intellectual property rights in materials, processes and systems used or created by ClearPath in the performance of the Services shall remain vested in ClearPath unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6.2 Any bespoke reporting templates or workflow documentation created specifically for the Client shall be owned jointly.

---

## 7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice to the other party after the expiry of the Initial Term.

7.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach of this Agreement that remains unremedied for 21 days after written notice specifying the breach has been given.

7.3 ClearPath shall not suspend Services for late payment unless payment is more than 60 days overdue and 14 days' written notice of intended suspension has been given.

---

## 8. Service Levels

8.1 ClearPath commits to the following minimum service standards:

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Next-day UK delivery success rate | 99.2% |
| Same-day London delivery (pre 10am orders) | 98.5% |
| Proof of delivery provided within | 2 hours of delivery |
| Customer query response time | Within 2 business hours |

8.2 Service credit of 5% of monthly charges shall apply for each full percentage point below target in any calendar month.

---

## 9. General

9.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and representations.

9.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales, and each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.

## Schedule 1 — Description of Services

- Collection and delivery of confidential legal documents across the United Kingdom
- Same-day delivery within the M25 corridor (orders placed before 10:00am)
- International document courier to EU and key jurisdictions (USA, UAE, Singapore)
- Tracked courier service with GPS monitoring and chain of custody documentation
- Secure document storage with climate-controlled archive facility
- Secure destruction with certificate of destruction provided within 24 hours
- Digital mailroom scanning and indexing service
- Monthly and ad-hoc management reporting suite
