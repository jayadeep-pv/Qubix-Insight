## Executive Summary

Crestview Partners Ltd ("Crestview") is a leading technology transformation consultancy specialising exclusively in the legal sector. With over 20 years of experience and a dedicated legal technology practice of more than 80 consultants, we have delivered LPMS implementations for 23 UK law firms — including seven firms of comparable size and complexity to Meridian Law Group.

We understand the pressures facing modern law firms: regulatory change, competitive pricing, fee earner productivity and client expectation. Our LPMS delivery framework, LegalEdge, has been refined through over two decades of legal sector engagements and reduces implementation risk significantly compared to bespoke development approaches.

## 1. Our Understanding of the Requirement

Meridian Law Group requires a modern, integrated LPMS that will:

- Manage cases across litigation, corporate and property practice areas
- Provide seamless time recording and billing, integrated with accounts
- Deliver a secure, branded client portal
- Embed SRA, AML and GDPR compliance workflows
- Produce actionable management information and KPI reporting
- Integrate with Microsoft 365 and the existing document management system
- Provide mobile access for fee earners in and out of the office

We have delivered all of these requirements for multiple comparable firms. Our proposal is built on seven engagements with firms of 100–350 fee earners — the profile closest to Meridian Law Group.

## 2. Proposed Solution

### 2.1 Platform

Crestview proposes deployment of its LegalEdge platform, a purpose-built LPMS framework for mid-to-large UK law firms. LegalEdge is built on Microsoft Azure (App Services, Azure SQL, Azure AD) with a React frontend and is already in production use at 14 UK law firms.

Rather than building from scratch, our approach provides Meridian with a battle-tested codebase that can be configured and extended to meet specific requirements — reducing risk and accelerating delivery.

### 2.2 Key Features

| Feature | Our Approach |
|---|---|
| Case management | LegalEdge core module — fully configurable by practice area and team |
| Time recording | Native time recording with Outlook add-in and mobile capture |
| Client portal | Branded portal with DocuSign integration for e-signatures |
| Compliance module | Pre-built AML, SRA and GDPR workflow engine with audit trail |
| Reporting | Embedded Power BI + pre-built legal KPI dashboard library (47 reports) |
| Mobile | Native iOS and Android app (in live use at 9 existing clients) |
| Integration | Microsoft Graph, RESTful APIs, and pre-built connectors for Elite 3E, Aderant and SOS |

## 3. Project Approach and Timeline

### 3.1 Methodology

Crestview delivers all LPMS projects using a structured configuration-led delivery model with two-week sprint cycles and weekly steering committee meetings. Our methodology is ISO 9001 certified and has been independently audited by three legal sector clients.

### 3.2 Proposed Timeline: 14 Months

| Phase | Description | Duration | End Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Discovery, configuration design and data analysis | 2 months | June 2024 |
| Phase 2 | Core LPMS configuration and integration build | 4 months | October 2024 |
| Phase 3 | Compliance module, portal and reporting | 3 months | January 2025 |
| Phase 4 | UAT, training and cutover preparation | 3 months | April 2025 |
| Phase 5 | Go-live, stabilisation and post-go-live support | 2 months | June 2025 |

Our 14-month timeline (vs. the industry average of 18–24 months) is achievable because of the pre-built LegalEdge foundation. We have delivered within this timeline on 6 of our last 7 comparable engagements.

### 3.3 Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Scope creep | Low | LegalEdge standard feature set covers ~85% of typical requirements |
| Data migration | Medium | Dedicated data migration workstream and pre-built ETL tooling |
| Change management | Low–Medium | Embedded change lead and dedicated firm champion programme |
| Integration delays | Low | Pre-built connectors for most common legal sector tools |
| Go-live readiness | Low | Structured go/no-go decision framework with clear criteria |
