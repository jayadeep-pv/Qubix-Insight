## Parties

**Service Provider:** Nexford Logistics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 08741293), having its registered office at 14 Faraday Way, Sheffield, S9 1XZ ("Nexford")

**Client:** Harrowfield & Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (OC No. 312847), having its principal place of business at One Aldersgate Street, London, EC2A 4HJ ("Client")

---

## 1. Services

1.1 Nexford agrees to provide nationwide document courier and logistics services as more particularly described in Schedule 1 attached hereto ("Services").

1.2 The Services shall commence on 1 April 2024 and shall continue for an initial term of two (2) years unless earlier terminated in accordance with Clause 7 ("Initial Term").

1.3 Nexford shall assign a dedicated Account Manager to the Client within five (5) business days of the commencement date.

---

## 2. Charges and Payment Terms

2.1 The Client shall pay Nexford the charges set out in Schedule 2, which amounts to £180,000 per annum exclusive of VAT.

2.2 Nexford shall issue invoices monthly in arrears on the last working day of each calendar month.

2.3 Payment terms: Net 60 days from the date of invoice. All payments shall be made by BACS transfer to the bank account notified by Nexford in writing.

2.4 Interest shall accrue on overdue amounts at 4% per annum above the Bank of England base rate, calculated daily.

2.5 Nexford reserves the right to increase charges annually by up to 5% or the rate of CPI (whichever is lower) on thirty (30) days' written notice.

---

## 3. Liability

3.1 Neither party excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability which cannot be excluded or limited by applicable law.

3.2 Nexford's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement, whether arising in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall in no event exceed £500,000.

3.3 Neither party shall be liable to the other for any loss of profits, loss of business, loss of goodwill, loss of anticipated savings, or any indirect or consequential loss.

---

## 4. Data Protection

4.1 Both parties acknowledge they are each independent data controllers in respect of any personal data processed in connection with this Agreement.

4.2 Each party shall comply in all respects with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

4.3 GDPR compliance clause: Included. Nexford has appointed a Data Protection Officer contactable at dpo@nexfordlogistics.co.uk. An up-to-date Record of Processing Activities is maintained and available upon request.

4.4 Nexford shall notify the Client within 48 hours of becoming aware of any personal data breach that may affect Client data.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and not to disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, save where disclosure is required by law or regulation.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All intellectual property rights in materials, processes and systems used or created by Nexford in the performance of the Services shall remain vested in Nexford unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

## 7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 14 days' written notice to the other party.

7.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach of this Agreement that remains unremedied for 14 days after written notice specifying the breach has been given.

7.3 Nexford may suspend the Services without liability if payment is more than 30 days overdue, following 7 days' written notice of such suspension.

---

## 8. General

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and representations.

8.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales, and each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.

## Schedule 1 — Description of Services

- Collection and delivery of confidential legal documents across the United Kingdom
- Same-day delivery available within the M25 corridor (orders placed before 10:00am)
- Tracked courier service with full chain of custody documentation
- Secure document storage for up to 30 days pending instruction
- Secure destruction of documents upon written instruction
- Monthly management reporting including volume, SLA compliance and exception logs
