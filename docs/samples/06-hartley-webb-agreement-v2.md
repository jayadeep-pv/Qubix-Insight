# SOFTWARE IMPLEMENTATION AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement Reference:** HW-SISA-2024-001
**Date:** 15 February 2024
**Version:** Draft v2.0 — Revised Following Client Negotiation

> This document reflects amendments agreed between the parties during negotiation meetings held on 29 January and 8 February 2024. Changes from v1.0 are tracked and summarised in the cover note issued separately. This version is subject to final board approval by both parties.

---

## Parties

**Supplier:** Hartley & Webb Technology Solutions Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 07341928), having its registered office at Hartley House, 22 King Street, Manchester, M2 6AQ ("Hartley & Webb")

**Client:** Meridian Law Group LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (OC No. 408293), having its principal place of business at 30 St Mary Axe, London, EC3A 8EP ("Client")

---

## 1. Scope of Services

1.1 Hartley & Webb shall design, develop and implement a bespoke Legal Practice Management System ("System") for the Client in accordance with the specification set out in Schedule 1.

1.2 The project shall be delivered in four phases over an estimated timeline of 18 months from the project commencement date ("Commencement Date"), with milestones as set out in Schedule 2.

1.3 Hartley & Webb shall provide post-go-live support and maintenance for a period of 24 months following system acceptance (extended from 12 months in v1.0).

---

## 2. Charges and Payment Terms

2.1 The total fixed price for implementation of the System is £485,000 exclusive of VAT, payable in accordance with the milestone payment schedule in Schedule 2.

2.2 Invoices shall be issued upon completion of each milestone as jointly certified by both parties' project managers.

2.3 Payment terms: Net 30 days from date of invoice (amended from Net 45 days in v1.0).

2.4 Interest on overdue invoices shall accrue at 4% above the Bank of England base rate (reduced from 8% in v1.0).

2.5 Any variations to the agreed scope of work shall require a signed Change Request and shall be charged at the agreed blended day rate of £1,100 per consultant day (reduced from £1,250 in v1.0).

---

## 3. Liability

3.1 Hartley & Webb's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed two times (2x) the total charges paid or payable by the Client under this Agreement (amended from 1x monthly equivalent in v1.0).

3.2 Based on the total contract value of £485,000, the effective liability cap is £970,000.

3.3 The following losses are excluded by both parties: loss of profits, loss of goodwill, loss of anticipated savings, and indirect or consequential loss not reasonably foreseeable at the time of entering this Agreement.

3.4 The exclusion in Clause 3.3 shall not apply in cases of wilful misconduct, fraud, or breach of confidentiality obligations (new clause added in v2.0 at Client's request).

---

## 4. Intellectual Property

4.1 All intellectual property rights in any bespoke work product created specifically for the Client under this Agreement, including all deliverables, customisations, configurations and associated documentation, shall vest in and be assigned to the Client upon full payment of all amounts due (amended from Supplier ownership in v1.0).

4.2 Hartley & Webb retains all rights in its pre-existing proprietary platform, frameworks, tools and methodologies ("Background IP") used in the delivery of the System.

4.3 Hartley & Webb grants the Client a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use the Background IP to the extent necessary to operate and maintain the System.

4.4 Upon termination or expiry, Hartley & Webb shall provide the Client with full source code escrow access within 30 days (new provision added in v2.0).

---

## 5. Data Protection

5.1 The parties acknowledge that Hartley & Webb acts as a data processor on behalf of the Client in respect of any personal data processed during implementation and support.

5.2 Hartley & Webb shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data in accordance with UK GDPR requirements. A Data Processing Agreement is attached as Schedule 3.

5.3 Hartley & Webb shall notify the Client of any personal data breach within 24 hours of discovery (new clause in v2.0).

---

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Both parties agree to keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party during the term of this Agreement and for a period of five (5) years following termination (extended from two years in v1.0).

---

## 7. Termination

7.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on giving 30 days' written notice to Hartley & Webb (reduced from 90 days in v1.0), provided that the Client shall remain liable for all work completed and costs incurred up to the date of termination. The cancellation fee of 25% of remaining contract value has been removed in this version.

7.2 Hartley & Webb may terminate this Agreement for cause on giving 30 days' written notice if: (a) the Client fails to make payment within 60 days of the due date and fails to remedy within a further 14 days of written notice; or (b) the Client commits an act of insolvency.

---

## 8. Warranty

8.1 Hartley & Webb warrants that the System will perform materially in accordance with the agreed specification for a period of 180 days following final system acceptance ("Warranty Period") (extended from 90 days in v1.0).

8.2 During the Warranty Period, Hartley & Webb shall remedy material defects within agreed SLA response times at no additional charge. Following expiry, remediation is covered under the annual support agreement.

---

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any dispute shall first be referred to senior management for resolution. Failing resolution within 30 days, disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts (arbitration clause removed at Client's request in v2.0).

## Schedule 1 — System Specification (Summary)

- Case management module for litigation, corporate and property matters
- Time recording and billing integrated with existing accounts software
- Client portal with secure document sharing
- Regulatory compliance module (SRA, AML, GDPR)
- Management reporting and KPI dashboards
- Integration with Microsoft 365 and existing document management system
- Mobile application for fee earners (iOS and Android)
- Business intelligence module with predictive matter profitability (added in v2.0)
