# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement Reference:** HP-MSA-2023-0047
**Date:** 3 October 2023
**Version:** 4.2 — Execution Copy

---

## Parties

**Service Provider:** Pinnacle Technology Group Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 06183920), having its registered office at Pinnacle House, 1 Bartholomew Lane, London, EC2N 2AX ("Pinnacle")

**Client:** Harrowfield & Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (OC No. 312847), having its principal place of business at One Aldersgate Street, London, EC2A 4HJ ("Client")

Together referred to as "the Parties" or individually as a "Party".

---

## Recitals

A. Pinnacle is in the business of providing technology consulting, software development, managed services and related professional services to organisations in the legal and professional services sectors.

B. The Client wishes to engage Pinnacle to provide various technology services from time to time, and Pinnacle is willing to provide such services, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

C. This Agreement establishes the master commercial framework governing all Statements of Work entered into between the Parties. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a Statement of Work, the terms of this Agreement shall prevail unless the Statement of Work expressly provides otherwise.

---

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

**"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

**"Authorised Users"** means the employees, contractors or agents of the Client who are authorised by the Client to access and use the Services.

**"Background IP"** means any intellectual property owned by or licensed to a Party prior to the commencement of a Statement of Work, or developed independently of the Services.

**"Change Request"** means a written request to modify the scope, timeline or cost of a Statement of Work, executed in accordance with Clause 9.

**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including technical data, trade secrets, business plans, financial information, client data, and proprietary methodologies.

**"Data Protection Legislation"** means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and all applicable guidance issued by the Information Commissioner's Office, as amended from time to time.

**"Deliverable"** means any work product, software, documentation, report or other output produced by Pinnacle pursuant to a Statement of Work.

**"Effective Date"** means 1 November 2023.

**"Force Majeure Event"** means any event beyond a Party's reasonable control including acts of God, pandemic, government action, cyberattack by a third party, or failure of third-party infrastructure.

**"Intellectual Property Rights"** means patents, copyrights, trade marks, database rights, design rights, trade secrets, know-how and all other intellectual property rights, whether registered or unregistered, in any jurisdiction.

**"Personal Data"** has the meaning given to it under the Data Protection Legislation.

**"Services"** means the services described in each Statement of Work.

**"Statement of Work"** or **"SOW"** means a written document executed by both Parties, incorporating this Agreement by reference, that describes a specific engagement including scope, deliverables, timeline, fees and any special terms.

---

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial period of three (3) years ("Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Clause 14.

2.2 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the relevant renewal date.

2.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement shall not affect the rights and obligations of the Parties under any Statement of Work then in effect, which shall continue to be governed by this Agreement until completion or separate termination.

---

## 3. Statements of Work

3.1 No Services shall be provided under this Agreement except pursuant to a mutually agreed and executed Statement of Work.

3.2 Each Statement of Work shall include: (a) a description of the Services; (b) the deliverables and acceptance criteria; (c) the project timeline and key milestones; (d) the fees and payment schedule; (e) the names of key personnel; and (f) any special terms modifying this Agreement for that engagement.

3.3 Either Party may propose a Statement of Work by providing a written draft to the other Party. The receiving Party shall respond within ten (10) business days.

---

## 4. Charges and Payment

4.1 The fees payable under each Statement of Work shall be as specified therein. Unless otherwise stated, all fees are exclusive of VAT, which shall be added at the prevailing rate.

4.2 Pinnacle shall invoice the Client in accordance with the payment schedule set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

4.3 Payment terms: All invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date ("Payment Due Date").

4.4 If the Client disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify Pinnacle in writing within 10 business days of receipt, specifying the disputed amount and the grounds for dispute. The Client shall pay all undisputed amounts by the Payment Due Date.

4.5 Interest shall accrue on overdue and undisputed amounts at 4% per annum above the Bank of England base rate, calculated on a daily basis from the Payment Due Date until the date of actual payment.

4.6 Pinnacle may, after providing 14 days' written notice, suspend performance of any or all Services where any undisputed amount remains unpaid for more than 45 days after the Payment Due Date.

4.7 Annual fee review: Pinnacle may increase its standard rates once per calendar year on not less than 60 days' written notice. Rate increases shall not apply to fixed-price Statements of Work that are already in effect.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property Rights

5.1 Background IP: Each Party retains ownership of its respective Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers or licenses Background IP except as expressly set out herein.

5.2 Pinnacle Background IP Licence: Pinnacle grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence to use Pinnacle's Background IP to the extent reasonably necessary to use and enjoy the Deliverables for the Client's internal business purposes.

5.3 Foreground IP — Bespoke Deliverables: Subject to full payment of all fees due, all Intellectual Property Rights in any bespoke Deliverable created specifically for the Client under a Statement of Work (and not incorporating Pinnacle Background IP) shall be assigned to the Client with effect from the date of completion of the relevant Statement of Work. Pinnacle shall execute any documents reasonably required to give effect to such assignment.

5.4 Foreground IP — Platform and Tools: Where a Deliverable incorporates or is derived from Pinnacle's proprietary platform, tools, frameworks or accelerators, those elements shall remain the property of Pinnacle and the Client shall receive a licence on the terms set out in Clause 5.2.

5.5 Open Source: Pinnacle shall maintain a record of any open source software incorporated into Deliverables. Pinnacle warrants that such use complies with all applicable open source licences and that the Client's use of Deliverables will not be restricted by any open source copyleft obligation.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, save where disclosure is required by law, regulation or court order.

6.2 Each Party shall use the other's Confidential Information solely for the purposes of performing or receiving the Services under this Agreement.

6.3 The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the receiving Party without reference to the disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is disclosed with the prior written approval of the disclosing Party.

6.4 The obligations of confidentiality shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

---

## 7. Data Protection

7.1 The Parties acknowledge that in performing the Services, Pinnacle will process Personal Data as a data processor on behalf of the Client as data controller.

7.2 Pinnacle shall process Personal Data only in accordance with the Client's documented instructions and shall not process Personal Data for any other purpose.

7.3 Pinnacle shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access.

7.4 Pinnacle shall assist the Client in complying with its obligations under the Data Protection Legislation, including in relation to data subject access requests, data protection impact assessments and notification of personal data breaches to the Information Commissioner's Office.

7.5 Pinnacle shall notify the Client in writing within 24 hours of becoming aware of any Personal Data Breach affecting Client Personal Data.

7.6 Upon termination or expiry of any Statement of Work, Pinnacle shall, at the Client's election, return or securely destroy all Personal Data within 30 days, and provide written confirmation of such deletion.

7.7 A Data Processing Agreement is appended as Schedule 1 and forms part of this Agreement.

---

## 8. Liability

8.1 Each Party's total aggregate liability to the other Party in connection with this Agreement, whether arising in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall not exceed the greater of: (a) the total fees paid or payable by the Client under the relevant Statement of Work during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim; or (b) £500,000.

8.2 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any: (a) loss of profits; (b) loss of revenue; (c) loss of anticipated savings; (d) loss of goodwill or reputation; (e) loss or corruption of data; or (f) indirect, special or consequential loss of any nature, in each case even if such loss was foreseeable or the Party had been advised of its possibility.

8.3 The limitations in this Clause 8 shall not apply to: (a) death or personal injury caused by negligence; (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (c) a Party's liability for breach of Clause 6 (Confidentiality); or (d) any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by applicable law.

8.4 Pinnacle holds professional indemnity insurance of £5,000,000 per claim and public liability insurance of £10,000,000 per occurrence. Evidence of such insurance shall be provided to the Client upon written request.

---

## 9. Change Control

9.1 Either Party may request a change to the scope, timeline or cost of a Statement of Work by submitting a written Change Request to the other Party.

9.2 Pinnacle shall, within five (5) business days of receiving a Change Request, provide an impact assessment setting out: (a) the proposed amendment to scope; (b) any adjustment to fees; (c) the impact on the project timeline; and (d) any other material implications.

9.3 A Change Request shall only take effect when signed by an authorised representative of each Party. Until then, Pinnacle shall continue to perform the Services in accordance with the original Statement of Work.

---

## 10. Warranties

10.1 Pinnacle warrants that: (a) it has the right, power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement; (b) the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care by suitably qualified personnel; (c) the Deliverables will materially conform to the requirements set out in the applicable Statement of Work; and (d) to the best of Pinnacle's knowledge, the Deliverables will not infringe any third-party Intellectual Property Rights.

10.2 The Client warrants that: (a) it has the right, power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement; and (b) all information and materials provided to Pinnacle are accurate and complete in all material respects.

---

## 11. Indemnity

11.1 Pinnacle shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Client from and against any third-party claim that the Deliverables (excluding any Client-supplied materials) infringe any third-party Intellectual Property Rights in the United Kingdom.

11.2 The Client shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Pinnacle from and against any third-party claim arising from: (a) the Client's use of the Deliverables in a manner inconsistent with this Agreement; or (b) inaccurate or incomplete information provided by the Client to Pinnacle.

---

## 12. Force Majeure

12.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that such delay or failure is caused by a Force Majeure Event, provided that the affected Party: (a) promptly notifies the other Party in writing; (b) uses reasonable endeavours to overcome or mitigate the effects of the Force Majeure Event; and (c) resumes performance as soon as reasonably practicable.

12.2 If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than 30 consecutive days, either Party may terminate the affected Statement of Work on 14 days' written notice without liability (other than for Services already performed and expenses already incurred).

---

## 13. Audit Rights

13.1 The Client may, on not less than 20 business days' written notice, audit Pinnacle's records, systems and procedures for the purpose of verifying Pinnacle's compliance with its obligations under this Agreement, including data protection obligations. Such audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and at the Client's cost, and shall not unreasonably disrupt Pinnacle's operations.

---

## 14. Termination

14.1 Termination for convenience: Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience on 60 days' written notice to the other Party, provided that the Client shall pay for all Services performed and costs incurred by Pinnacle up to the date of termination.

14.2 Termination for cause: Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work with immediate effect upon written notice if: (a) the other Party commits a material breach that remains unremedied for 30 days after written notice; (b) the other Party becomes insolvent, enters administration, or makes arrangements with its creditors; or (c) a Force Majeure Event continues for more than 90 days.

14.3 Consequences of termination: Upon termination: (a) all accrued rights and liabilities shall survive; (b) Pinnacle shall deliver to the Client all completed Deliverables and work in progress; (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information; and (d) Pinnacle shall provide reasonable transition assistance for a period of up to 60 days at its standard rates.

---

## 15. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 The Parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute in good faith through escalation to senior management within 20 business days of written notice of a dispute.

15.3 If the dispute is not resolved through senior management escalation, either Party may refer the matter to mediation under the CEDR Model Mediation Procedure.

15.4 If mediation fails to resolve the dispute within 30 days of the mediator's appointment, the dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.

---

## 16. General

16.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement (together with all Statements of Work and schedules) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all prior representations, agreements and understandings.

16.2 Variation: No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

16.3 Waiver: Failure to exercise or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that right.

16.4 Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, it shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

16.5 Assignment: Neither Party may assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign to an Affiliate without consent, provided the assigning Party remains liable.

16.6 Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be sent by email (with read receipt requested) or by recorded delivery to the addresses set out on the signature page.

16.7 No Partnership: Nothing in this Agreement shall create a partnership, joint venture or employment relationship between the Parties.

16.8 Third Party Rights: This Agreement does not confer any rights on any third party under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.
